# First-visit intro: artwork

The intro's flip-book (`src/components/layout/Intro.tsx`) shows ancient Greek sport art, cropped to small cards by
`scripts/intro-cards.mjs` (the original photos are kept in `media-src/intro-art/`, not committed). Every photo comes
from a museum open-access programme and is in the **public domain** — free to use, including commercially, with no
permission needed. Credit is not required, but is given here.
